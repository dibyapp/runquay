How Runquay works
  1. Add a task. Say what you want and what a good result looks like.
  2. Start work. One AI tool works at a time. Pause whenever you need.
  3. Review the result. Check the files and tests before accepting or publishing.

Work happens in small steps. Progress is saved so another eligible account can continue. Your computer must stay on and connected.

About accounts and usage

There is no account-count cap. Each separate account needs its own vendor sign-in. Adding profiles does not increase allowance. Codex can move to another eligible Codex account; other tools ask before each run. Credentials stay with the vendor tool. Removing a connection keeps its credentials and history.

    Which AI tool will you use?
    Runquay needs a small helper program from your AI provider. Having a chat account alone is not enough. Installed means the helper is on this computer; sign-in is checked next.
    What if I have never used a terminal?
    A terminal is an app where you type instructions for your computer: PowerShell on Windows, Terminal on Mac or Linux. Open the Installation guide for your AI tool and follow its official steps. You only need this for setup; everyday tasks use the buttons here. Runquay does not install the helper or buy a subscription for you.
    Custom commands extend Runquay to other headless tools. GUI-only products require a CLI bridge; they cannot be automated by name alone.
